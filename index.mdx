---
title: "Introduction"
description: "Reelhaus is a movie database API for films, cast and crew, audience reviews, and rankings."
icon: "film"
---

Reelhaus gives you programmatic access to a catalog of films and the people who make them. Search movies, pull cast and crew, read and post audience reviews, and compare how films perform with critics versus at the box office.

<Note>
  Reelhaus is a fictional API built for demonstration purposes. Requests to `api.reelhaus.example` won't resolve.
</Note>
